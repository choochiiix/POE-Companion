# Conversion and incoming hit scenarios

`conversion.ts` splits unscaled damage into ordered physical → lightning → cold → fire → chaos paths. Each path retains its original damage-type history. Applicable increased modifiers sum, applicable more modifiers multiply, and generic/elemental modifiers apply once per path. `gain` copies source damage without removing it; `convert` reduces retained damage. Flat typed damage belongs to its original type before conversion. Path fractions remain continuous, not game-display rounded. This follows the type-history approach in the pinned PoB `CalcOffence.lua` `calcDamage` implementation; these tests are hand-derived, not full Lua offence differential tests.

The caller resolves skill/global conversion priority. Totals above 100% throw rather than guessing PoB priority/normalization. Reverse conversion, arbitrary conversion exceptions, damage-over-time and ailment source damage remain unsupported. Aggregate endpoint `base/increased/more` fields are summaries, with `increased=0` and `more=1`; inspect `conversionPaths` for exact per-path factors. The sum of endpoint values is authoritative. Flat BASE contributions are retained alongside the scaling contributions; their raw values precede path fractions.

`advanced-defence.ts` takes an explicit already-landed incoming hit and resolved defence. Damage-taken-as is simultaneous and nonchaining, applied before armour/resistance. It computes unblocked mitigation, suppressed spell mitigation and expected damage under independent block and suppression rolls. Block chance is a final effective probability (caller resolves caps and special rules). Block normally takes zero damage; `blockedDamagePercent` supports explicitly normalized partial damage. Suppression defaults to 50% prevention when not specified. Attack hits ignore suppression. Armour depends on the physical portion after shifting.

This is an expected single-hit scenario, not EHP, maximum-hit, recovery or resource-pool simulation. No evasion roll, guard skills, damage prevention pools, ally redirection, MoM, ES bypass, recharge, leech, ailment avoidance, keystone parsing or DoT is inferred. Special mechanics must be declared unsupported by source adapters. Formula references: pinned PoB CalcDefence armourReductionF, applyDmgTakenConversion and SpellSuppressionEffect. Tests are independent arithmetic fixtures rather than full PoB defensive environment runs.
