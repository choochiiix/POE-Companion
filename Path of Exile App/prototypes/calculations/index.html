Scope matters

One hit per cast. No cost or uptime limit. Selected conditions must be explicit; unsupported mechanics stay visible.

Additional context for future integration

Skill parts and stages, active instance counts, buffs and charges, curses, ailment state, incoming hit type/size, block and suppression, trigger cadence, reservation and recovery.
