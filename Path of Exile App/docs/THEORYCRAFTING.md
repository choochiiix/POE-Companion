# Theorycrafting

Theorycrafting is a local-first discovery workspace. Choose a supported active
skill gem or a unique weapon/armour variant to see mechanics that may fit it.
Recommendations keep source identities, explain their interaction, and show
conditions. This first milestone is a shortlist for ideas to try; it is not a
whole-build optimizer or a claim that the pinned snapshots match the live game.

## First milestone

The starter scenario works without a saved character: level 90 Witch, no
allocated passives or mastery choices, and 0% enemy resistance. A saved build
is not required. Changing mode or a selection only updates the preview; it does
not write gear, skills, passives, ascendancy, or another save.

The seeded spell examples are Fireball, Arc, Spark, Ball Lightning, and
Lightning Conduit where the local source rules identify a direct hit. For
Fireball, Heart of Flame is sourced as 30% increased Fire Damage and 6% Fire
Resistance penetration. Mastermind of Discord is shown as conditional on
inflicting Exposure; the starter does not assume Exposure is already present.
Fire Mastery options are individual effects and require an allocated node in
the mastery cluster. Only mastery effects with an exact, source-backed rule
are ranked; unrelated weapon, defensive, or unsupported conversion effects do
not appear as skill synergies. The pinned Corrosive Elements text describes a
25% chance for Fire Skills to inflict Fire Exposure on Hit, but node 2306 has no
links in the pinned main-tree graph. It is shown as an unpriced source-text
example with an explicit path limitation, not as a reachable passive choice.

Doryani's Prototype is the seeded unique-first conditional example. Its
recommendations use the selected current variant's pinned text, including the
non-Lightning damage restriction, the nearby enemy resistance interaction,
and the distinction between lightning hits and damage over time. The current
calculator does not model those material rules, so no numeric result is shown
for this item. Other numeric unique comparisons are displayed only when the
same skill and encounter assumptions can be evaluated with the supported
calculator effects; the comparison names its metric, item comparator, and
assumptions. Unsupported effects stay visible as conditions or diagnostics.

Support suggestions use the imported PoB skill-type rules to reject explicit
incompatibilities. When the local records cannot prove full compatibility,
the result says it is unverified rather than treating a tag match as proof.
Passive recommendations use the local tree graph and class roots. Their costs
include travel nodes. Mastery choices include the cluster prerequisite, and
ascendancy costs are shown separately from ordinary passive points. Each
recommendation is costed on its own path; do not add point totals across
suggestions because their travel nodes may overlap. Already allocated nodes
cost zero; unreachable targets keep their reason on the recommendation card and
receive no invented point cost. This avoids repeating every unreachable
candidate in a long page-level diagnostic list.

Evidence labels distinguish calculated hit results, conditional mechanics,
and rule-based text/tag matches. Conditions describe what the setup needs to
do. A number is omitted when an unsupported effect could materially change
the selected metric.

## Pinned inputs and limits

- Skills and support rules come from `crafting-ui/skills-data.json`, generated
  by `scripts/build-skills.py` from the preserved
  `references/path-of-building/PathOfBuilding-dev.zip` archive. Its pinned
  manifest is PoB 2.67.2; the archive hash is recorded in
  `calculation-engine/reference/pin.json`. The imported file contains 559
  active gems and 263 supports. This is source evidence, not a live-patch
  guarantee.
- Unique variants come from `crafting-ui/catalog-data.json`, generated by
  `scripts/build-catalog.mjs` from the static PoB unique definitions plus the
  supplied RePoE and identity/flavour data. The catalog currently contains
  1,307 definitions and 3,250 variant records. The RePoE PoE 1 export is
  labelled 3.29.3.6 in `data-sources/repoe-poe1/README.md`. Static definitions
  and untranslated mechanics leave gaps; preserve the report and its hashes.
- Passive nodes come from `data-sources/pob-tree-3_29/tree.json`, generated
  from the pinned PoB 3.29 tree source by `scripts/build-tree.mjs`. The local
  snapshot has 3,397 nodes. Keep the tree source, hashes, artwork, and license
  together when refreshing it.
- The numeric engine's recorded reference is PoB Community 2.68.1. Its
  `fireball-penetration` fixture uses a level 1 Fireball record, a level 20
  Witch, 75% enemy Fire Resistance, 20% penetration, 10% Fire Exposure, and
  the matching 3.29 passive tree. The recorded Effective DPS result is 13.53.
  See `calculation-engine/reference/v2.68.1/README.md` and `fixtures.json` for
  the exact inputs and coverage. It validates that matching hit scenario only;
  it does not validate every Theorycrafting recommendation or unique effect.

Do not update generated JSON by hand. Follow each generator's pinned inputs,
hashes, and tests. Snapshot records may mix source revisions, so retain the
source label and limitations with each recommendation.

## Extending the rules

`crafting-ui/features/theorycrafting/index-model.js` normalizes the local
records while preserving their exact IDs and provenance. Add reusable,
declarative interaction predicates there. Candidate retrieval may use tags,
but validate actor, delivery, direct-hit or damage-over-time behavior, damage
type, support compatibility, required weapon/slot, and any conditional mechanic
before producing a result. Keep UI wording out of the source data and add a
focused fixture to `crafting-ui/test/theorycrafting-index.test.mjs` for every
new interaction and its negative match.

`crafting-ui/features/theorycrafting/ranking-model.js` applies those rules,
prices legal tree paths, and prepares comparable numeric results only for
supported effects. Extend its colocated tests for path prerequisites, point
budgets, conditions, unsupported-result suppression, stable ordering, and
baseline/alternative equivalence. Keep numeric comparisons separate from
qualitative rule-based ordering.

Useful checks from the app directory:

```powershell
node --test crafting-ui/test/theorycrafting-index.test.mjs
node --test crafting-ui/features/theorycrafting/ranking-model.test.mjs
npm run typecheck
npm test
```

From `renderer/`, run `pnpm typecheck` and `pnpm build`. For isolated browser
checks, run `node scripts/test-react-theorycrafting-qa.mjs` from the app
directory; the script uses its own browser storage and checks desktop and
narrow layouts. To rerun the pinned representative calculation, use
`node --experimental-strip-types calculation-engine/reference/v2.68.1/compare.mjs`
from the app directory. A passing calculation fixture does not replace the
feature's applicability and UI checks.

## Future milestones

Keep these expansions separate from the first milestone and add pinned PoB
fixtures before presenting unsupported mechanics as calculated:

1. **Broader attacks and ailments.** Extend actor, weapon, hit, critical,
   ailment, and attack-delivery rules; refresh the relevant gem, support, item,
   and tree snapshots together. Add matching PoB cases for representative
   weapon classes and each supported ailment, with exact gem levels, quality,
   weapon rolls, enemy defences, and the metric being compared.
2. **Conditional delivery and sustain.** Add triggers, totems, traps/mines,
   charges, resource costs, recovery, and sustain conditions after their
   delivery model is explicit. Refresh pinned sources and add PoB fixtures for
   trigger timing, delivery counts, resource use, and each recovery loop under
   fixed encounter assumptions.
3. **Multi-source chains.** Model linked interactions such as conversion,
   triggers, recoup, and defensive loops as ordered, source-aware chains.
   Refresh all participating snapshots and add fixtures that compare each
   chain stage, final hit/DoT result, and defensive outcome against the same
   pinned PoB build, including negative cases where a condition is absent.

For every milestone, record the PoB release/archive hash, the game-data
snapshot labels, exact setup and encounter inputs, metric mapping, unsupported
effects, and tolerance. Do not describe historical fixture evidence as proof
of current-patch completeness.
